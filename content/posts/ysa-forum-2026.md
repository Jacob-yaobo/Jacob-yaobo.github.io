On May 13, 2026, [Xinwei Li](/team/li-xinwei/) and [Zeyang Wang](/team/wang-zeyang/) presented at the YSA Forum at the Medical University of Vienna.

## Xinwei Li

![Xinwei Li presenting at the YSA Forum](/images/news/20260513-ysa-xinwei-li.jpg)

Xinwei Li presented our team's study introducing a health-referenced approach to evaluate whole-body metabolic coordination using [18F]FDG PET. The study showed that obesity and heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF) are associated with disrupted inter-organ metabolic relationships, reflecting systemic metabolic reorganization beyond isolated organ dysfunction. These coordination changes were linked to clinical markers and cardiovascular outcomes, suggesting that whole-body FDG PET may help identify disease-related metabolic heterogeneity earlier and more comprehensively.

## Zeyang Wang

![Zeyang Wang presenting at the YSA Forum](/images/news/20260513-ysa-zeyang-wang.jpg)

Zeyang Wang presented our team's study using whole-body [18F]FDG PET/CT to evaluate metabolic changes after bariatric surgery. The study showed coordinated metabolic remodeling across multiple organs, including adipose tissue, liver, pancreas, spleen, adrenal glands, and skeletal muscle. These organ-level changes were associated with improvements in metabolic and inflammatory markers, highlighting molecular imaging as a tool for monitoring metabolic health and supporting personalized follow-up care after bariatric surgery.
